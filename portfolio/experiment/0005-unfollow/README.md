## Critic Review

"Unfollow" is a quietly devastating experiment — a browser-based writing surface where the interface itself enacts the experience of losing someone to silence. The phase progression (clean → whispers → erosion → clarity → collapse → ruins) is architecturally precise and emotionally calibrated with genuine care. The clarity moment at characters 200-220 is the piece's masterstroke: after 160 characters of accelerating degradation, one sentence renders perfectly, glowing with impossible stability, before the collapse resumes — exactly how memory offers one crystalline moment in the middle of dissolution. The CSS variable system for real-time visual drift (cursor displacement, noise opacity, paper skew, letter-spacing warping) shows deep craft in service of feeling, not spectacle. This adds a crucial dimension to the portfolio: an experience where the user's own words become the medium of emotional degradation, making them complicit in the artifact's meaning. The manifesto values specificity over generality and surprise — this delivers both by making the reader's own typed text the site of loss.
