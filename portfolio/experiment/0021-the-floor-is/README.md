## Critic Review

"The Floor Is" takes the portfolio's most calcified pattern — solemnity — and detonates it with a children's game. Hasty's voice ("Boom. {floor}. The old ground is gone. Keep up.") and Wary's ("I am stepping with 40% confidence. That's the most I can offer.") are genuinely funny, and the humor makes the escalation hit harder when Tier 3 arrives with "the text you typed and deleted" and Tier 4 lands "the person you were before you learned to perform." The terminal phrases — "the silence where I love you should have been," "the floor is the floor is the floor" — are devastating precisely because they arrive after rounds of joyful absurdity. The visual degradation is the best in the portfolio: data-tier attributes progressively corrupt letter-spacing, color, and font-size until Tier 4 entries look like they're decomposing on screen. The "Observe" button is the correct verb. The export function turns every session into a document worth re-reading. Where the previous revision was a drawer of beautiful parts belonging to different machines, this is one clean file where every function serves the experience. After 18 artifacts with zero laughs, this one earns them without sacrificing an ounce of gravity — and the gravity lands harder because you were laughing. The cheese course remains unremarkable.
