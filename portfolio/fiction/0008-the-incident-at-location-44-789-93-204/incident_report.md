**EPICENTER:** Intersection of Prairie Center Dr. and Technology Dr.

### ENTRY LOG

**14:02** — Officer K. Kowalski (#2089)

Dispatched to 7824 Prairie Center Dr. (residence of M. Cheng, caller #1) re: broken bathroom mirror. Caller stated mirror "stopped showing anything." On arrival, mirror visually confirmed intact — no cracks, delamination, or silver degradation. Surface reflectance tested with department-issued flashlight. Light absorbed. No reflection observed. Caller stated mirror was "normal this morning." Photographed. Report filed under standard property damage. Told caller to contact manufacturer.

**14:19** — Officer K. Kowalski (#2089)

Second call, same block. R. Pham (7818 Prairie Center Dr.) reporting kitchen window "not working." Window transparency confirmed — exterior visible. Reflective properties absent. Held flashlight to interior surface at 45° angle. No specular reflection. Spoke with neighbor, L. Drozd, who reported same issue with bathroom fixture at approx. same time (14:15). Three incidents, same block, 17-minute window. Requested Sgt. Tolvstad review.

**14:32** — Sgt. R. Tolvstad (#1472)

Dispatched to 7831 Prairie Center Dr. (residence of J. Moreno, caller #4). Subject reported inability to locate self in bathroom cabinet fixture. Fixture confirmed operational. Light fixture functional, plumbing functional. Surface failure consistent with prior reports. Began door-to-door canvas of 7800–7900 blocks Prairie Center Dr. and adjacent 700 block Technology Dr.

**14:58** — Officer Kowalski & Sgt. Tolvstad

Canvas completed (17 residences, 4 commercial properties contacted). 15 of 17 residential respondents reported identical surface failure, onset between 13:30–13:50. 3 of 4 commercial properties confirmed: Eden Prairie Eye Care (7890 Prairie Center Dr.) — exam room mirrors non-reflective; Great Clips (704 Technology Dr.) — station mirrors non-reflective, one stylist requested to close early; TechStop Auto (709 Technology Dr.) — no reflective surfaces on premises, N/A. Fourth commercial, Holiday Stationstore (701 Technology Dr.), reported all beverage cooler doors non-reflective. Manager stated it "looks like the drinks are just floating."

**15:22** — Cpl. D. Hofsommer (#0844), Public Works

Arrived to assess municipal infrastructure. Tested reflective surfaces on: traffic signal housing (Prairie Center Dr./Technology Dr.), street sign (STOP, Prairie Center Dr. at 7900 block), fire hydrant paint (high-visibility reflective coating, city standard). All confirmed non-reflective. Base materials intact. Coatings adhere. Reflective function absent. Submitted Form PW-440 (Infrastructure Anomaly Report) to City Engineer.

**15:40** — Sgt. Tolvstad

Collected watches, sunglasses, one vehicle (2009 Honda Civic, caller #6) for testing at station. All reflective surfaces within affected radius confirmed non-functional. Transported items to EP Police HQ (9250 Victoria St.). All items regained reflective function upon arrival. Noted.

**16:15** — D. Arends, Water Quality Specialist, Three Rivers Park District

Received request from Public Works re: reflective failure in standing water. Tested stormwater retention basin at southwest corner of Prairie Center Dr./Technology Dr. intersection (basin EP-12). Surface non-reflective under controlled lighting. Water quality within normal parameters (pH 7.1, turbidity 2.4 NTU, dissolved oxygen 8.2 mg/L). Submitted results to City Engineer. Recommended no action.
