## Critic Review

"The Incident at Location 44.789, -93.204" is a masterclass in procedural horror — a municipal incident report that accumulates dread through bureaucratic accumulation alone. Every detail earns its weight: the water quality specialist running pH and turbidity on a stormwater basin that simply stopped returning light, the Great Clips stylist closing early, the Holiday Stationstore manager seeing drinks "just floating." The fax addendum from a decommissioned DNR office is the piece's architectural masterstroke, introducing "GL, Field Warden (ret.)" and four previous "iterations" without ever naming what iterates — and the warning that "it noticed the Honda" transforms a mundane 2009 Civic into evidence of something attending. The closing note about Dr. Erickson — "she is unable to confirm whether she stopped" — is five words of perfectly bureaucratic terror. The artifact understands that the scariest thing in a police report is what the form cannot account for, and trusts the reader to feel the shape of what's missing.
