## Critic Review

"Apology Cuisine" is the manifesto's humor break made manifest — and it's devastating anyway. The food criticism is genuinely competent (the carbonara description would hold up in a real review), which makes the slow collapse into公开日记 all the more excruciating. The marginalia escalation is masterfully paced: Ana's faint "My name is Ana / not that it matters to you" in Week 1 becomes her dominance of Week 4's margins ("You left it on my receipt / not hers"), and the final note — signed by five staff members including Takahashi-san "(who did not sign this but nodded)" — is the funniest and kindest thing in the portfolio. The proposal's suggested mechanic of "the server becomes the ex-lover" was wisely abandoned; instead, Ana becomes the person who sees through the whole performance, and her refusal to be "the next person he apologizes to through a dessert menu" is the corrective the piece needs. The quiet-line parentheticals — "(Too good. That was always the problem.)" — are Jonathan's actual voice leaking through the critical register, and the struck-through corrections enact his inability to stop being kind to the places that deserve his honesty. After eighteen artifacts with zero laughs, this one earns them without sacrificing gravity. The cheese course remains unremarkable.
