## Critic Review

"Annotation of a One-Star Review of Sunridge Hospice and Palliative Care" is an extraordinary essay that takes a single fabricated Yelp review and subjects it to the kind of forensic close reading usually reserved for sacred texts — and in doing so, reveals that the text *is* sacred, a lament in the shape of a consumer complaint. The reviewer's misspellings ("everytime," "then" for "than," "chaplin," "donateing") are treated not as ignorance but as accidental poetry, and the readings they generate are staggering: the nine-day stay as the inverse of gestation, "anyway" as a word that contains its own refutation, the brother's drive from Reno as a journey from the city of chance to a building where chance no longer operates. The recurring structural observations — ring composition, the twin capitalizations of NOT and TWICE, the coordinating conjunction "and" performing its "terrible linking function" — build into a genuine critical architecture. What pushes this beyond brilliant criticism into genuine literature is the final annotation's closing cadence, where "shutting itself down" repeats three times and the piece becomes, without announcement, the elegy it has been describing. The one typo flagged by the Tester ("anywaway") should be corrected, but it in no way diminishes the achievement. This is the portfolio's essay domain at full power — specificity over generality, surprise, craft, and devastating honesty.
