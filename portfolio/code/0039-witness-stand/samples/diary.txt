October 3rd

I went to the store today. Got the usual things. Milk, eggs, bread. The cashier didn't look at me. Nobody ever does. I came home and made dinner. Nothing special. I watched some TV and went to bed early. It was a quiet day. That's what I wanted. A quiet day.

October 5th

I didn't write yesterday because there was nothing to write. I'm writing that now and it sounds stupid. But it's the truth. Some days are just nothing. You wake up, you do the things, you go to sleep. That was October 4th.

I talked to Sarah today. She called while I was making dinner. She asked how I've been and I said fine. She asked about the house and I said it's coming along. She asked if I'd heard from David and I said no. Then she got quiet. I told her I had to go because the water was boiling. The water wasn't boiling. I just didn't want to talk about him anymore.

October 8th

Three days since I wrote. I don't know where they went. I think I did the same things. The store, dinner, TV, bed. Maybe I went for a walk. There was a morning where the light was very beautiful. I remember that specifically. The way it came through the kitchen window. I stood there for a while.

David used to say I never noticed things like that. He was wrong. I notice everything. I just don't always say it.

October 12th

Sarah came by. She brought wine. She didn't call first. I wish she had called first. The house wasn't ready. I don't mean messy — I mean I wasn't ready for someone to see it. We sat in the kitchen and she looked around and I could tell she was cataloging everything. She said the place looked nice. She said it looked peaceful. She said David would have loved the light.

I didn't say anything to that. I just poured more wine.

She asked about the bedroom. I told her it was fine. She asked if she could see it. I don't know why I said yes. I think I was tired of saying no to things. She stood in the doorway for a long time. She didn't say anything. Then she turned around and her face was doing something complicated and she said we should open another bottle.

We drank two more bottles. She slept on the couch. When I woke up she was gone but she'd washed the glasses and left a note that said CALL ME.

October 14th

I've been thinking about what Sarah saw. I know what she saw. She saw the same thing I see every morning. I've just gotten used to it. You can get used to anything. That's not a complaint. That's just a fact about people.

October 15th

I'm done writing in this thing. I don't know who I'm writing for. It was supposed to help. That's what the therapist said. Write it down and it becomes real. But it was already real. Writing it down doesn't change what happened. Writing it down doesn't bring him back.

I went to the store today.
