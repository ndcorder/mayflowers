MERIDIAN SYSTEMS ANNOUNCES STRATEGIC WORKFORCE REALIGNMENT TO POSITION COMPANY FOR NEXT PHASE OF GROWTH

AUSTIN, TX — October 15, 2024 — Meridian Systems Corporation (NYSE: MSC), a global leader in enterprise workflow solutions, today announced a strategic workforce realignment initiative designed to better serve evolving market demands and position the company for sustainable long-term value creation.

As part of this initiative, certain positions across select divisions will be restructured. The company expects these changes to be substantially complete by the end of the fiscal year.

"This was not a decision made lightly," said Patricia Harmon, Chief Executive Officer of Meridian Systems. "Our people are the foundation of everything we do, and we are committed to treating every affected team member with dignity and respect throughout this process. We are providing comprehensive transition support including extended benefits coverage, outplacement services, and dedicated career counseling resources."

The realignment primarily impacts the company's Legacy Infrastructure division and certain support functions. Approximately 840 roles will be affected. Affected team members were notified earlier this week and will receive a minimum of 12 weeks of transition support.

These difficult but necessary steps reflect Meridian's commitment to operational excellence and its responsibility to all stakeholders — including our team members, our customers, our partners, and our shareholders — to maintain competitive positioning in an increasingly dynamic marketplace.

The decision follows a comprehensive review of organizational structure and operational needs, initiated as part of the company's previously announced Forward Meridian strategy launched in Q1 2024. This review was conducted by an independent cross-functional assessment team over a period of several months.

Meridian Systems remains committed to its core mission of empowering organizations through intelligent workflow technology and will continue to invest in key growth areas including cloud architecture, machine learning operations, and customer success.

ABOUT MERIDIAN SYSTEMS CORPORATION
Meridian Systems Corporation (NYSE: MSC) is a global technology company providing enterprise workflow solutions to more than 2,800 organizations across 40 countries. Founded in 2006, Meridian is headquartered in Austin, Texas with offices in Denver, Dublin, and Singapore. For more information, visit www.meridiansys.com.
